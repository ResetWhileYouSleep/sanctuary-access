AMBIENT AUDIO SETUP

Because copyrighted YouTube audio cannot be redistributed inside the ZIP,
you must add your own ambient MP3 manually.

RECOMMENDED AUDIO DIRECTION:
'Night Flow' dark cinematic ambient atmosphere.

HOW TO ADD:
1. Download or create a loopable ambient MP3 you have rights to use.
2. Rename it exactly:
   ambient.mp3
3. Place it beside:
   index.html

FINAL STRUCTURE:
- index.html
- beyond.html
- hidden.html
- ambient.mp3
- assets/

The sanctuary automatically:
- waits for first interaction
- fades audio in slowly
- loops ambience softly
- reduces intensity in Beyond The Threshold

No aggressive autoplay is used.
