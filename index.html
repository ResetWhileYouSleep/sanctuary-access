✦ Experience The Sanctuary ✦

The Sanctuary is designed to accompany your Reset While You Sleep journey.

Rather than rushing through every scene at once, allow the Sanctuary to unfold gradually over time.

You may return whenever you wish:

• Return To Stillness when you need a quiet pause.

• Visit Ritual Fragments for gentle reflections and reminders.

• Explore Evening Atmosphere when preparing for rest.

• Step through The Threshold when you feel ready to continue your journey.

There is no correct pace.

Some travelers stay for only a moment. Others return night after night.

The Sanctuary is meant to be experienced, not completed.

The path remains here, quietly waiting whenever you need it.
